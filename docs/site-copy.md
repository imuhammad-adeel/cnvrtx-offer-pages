How to read it:

- `[H1]`, `[H2]`, `[H3]` are headings. `[button]`, `[link]`, `[label]` and `[opens]` (a row that expands) say what the text sits on.
- `→ /path` after a link is where it goes.
- _(shown on interaction)_ is text that appears after a click, a scroll or a change in the calculator.
- When a block is word-for-word the same as one already listed, it says where to find it instead of repeating it.
