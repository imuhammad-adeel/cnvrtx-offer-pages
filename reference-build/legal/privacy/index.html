Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This is a draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet.

This policy explains what information CNVRT X LLC ("CNVRT X", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website and the CNVRT X Front Office service.

1Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Your name, business name, email address and phone number, when you reserve a build, check out or contact us.
  • Details about your shop that you provide for setup, such as services, hours, prices and policies.
  • Billing details. Payments are handled by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic device and usage data, such as pages viewed and buttons clicked.
  • Where you came from, such as campaign tags in a link (for example utm_source).

Information about your customers:

  • When we run the service for your shop, the system handles information about your customers, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, messages, bookings and job details. We process this on your behalf. It stays yours.

2How we use information

  • To set up, run and support the service you bought.
  • To reply to you and send account and setup messages.
  • To send tips and offers, only if you agreed to receive them.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal obligations and prevent misuse.

3Text messaging

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we use it only for the purpose you agreed to. Consent to receive texts is optional and is not a condition of buying anything.

Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with, sold to or rented to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

See our SMS Terms for how to stop messages and get help.

4When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the service, such as hosting, messaging, phone, payment and analytics providers. They may use it only to provide their service to us.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.
  • As part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

5Your shop’s customer data

Your domain, content and customer list belong to you. You can ask for an export at any time. We do not contact your customers for our own purposes and we do not sell or share their information.

6How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal, tax and accounting needs. You can ask us to delete information sooner, subject to those needs.

7Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law. Contact us and we will respond as the law requires.

9Children

This service is for businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will tell account holders by email.

11Contact

Contact CNVRT X LLC through the contact link in the footer of this website.
