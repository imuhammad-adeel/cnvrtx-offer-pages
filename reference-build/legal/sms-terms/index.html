SMS Terms

Effective November 16, 2026

This is a draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet.

These terms cover text messages sent by CNVRT X LLC ("CNVRT X") to people who agreed to receive them.

1What you are signing up for

We offer two text programs. Each is optional and each needs its own consent:

  • Account and setup texts: messages about your account, your build and your launch.
  • Tips and offers: occasional marketing messages about our services.

You agree by ticking the matching box on our form. Ticking a box is not required to reserve a build or to buy anything.

2How often we text

Message frequency varies. Account and setup texts are sent when there is something to tell you. Tips and offers are occasional.

3Cost

We do not charge for texts. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

4How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from that program. You will get one confirmation message. You can opt back in by replying START or by contacting us.

5How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact us. Contact CNVRT X LLC through the contact link in the footer of this website.

6Carriers

Texts are sent through US mobile carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed.

7Your privacy

Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with, sold to or rented to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

8Texts your shop sends to its customers

If you are a CNVRT X customer, the system can send texts to your own customers on your behalf, such as replies, confirmations and reminders. Those texts go only to people who agreed to receive them from your shop, and only after carrier approval. Your customers can reply STOP at any time.
