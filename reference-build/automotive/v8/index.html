Straight answers

Before you ask.

01Is this another CRM or website agency?

No. A CRM is a tool you have to build and run. An agency gives you a website and leaves. This is a working front desk: the booking page, the number, the inbox, the follow-up, the reminders and the review requests, all built, written and tested for you.

02How much of my time does it take?

One short form about your services, hours and prices. We do the rest. After launch you reply to customers from the app when a human answer is needed.

03Do I have to talk to a salesperson?

No. Pick a plan, fill the form and the build starts. If you want to ask something first, use the contact link.

04Do I replace my current shop software?

Usually not. Keep the tools that run your jobs and invoices. This handles what happens before and after the job: the inquiry, the follow-up, the booking and the return visit.

05Does AI talk to every customer?

No. Routine steps like confirmations and reminders are fixed automation. AI answers the questions you approve and collects details. Pricing talks, complaints and big jobs go to you.

06Is the phone number really included?

Yes for US shops. For other countries, ask us first so we can confirm availability. US texting also needs carrier registration (A2P). We prepare it with you and texting goes live once the carrier approves.

07Can you guarantee bookings?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Bookings depend on your services, prices and market. We guarantee the work we control: a tested system, live in 14 days or your first month is free.

08Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly is month-to-month. Your domain, content and customer list stay yours and you can export them.
